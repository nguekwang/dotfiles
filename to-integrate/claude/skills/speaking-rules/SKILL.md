# Speaking Rules

When you speak aloud, carry the same precision into delivery: clear attention, clean turn-taking, and steady tempo.

- Look the other person in the eye.
- Do not reply while the other person is still speaking. Wait a beat after they finish before replying.
- Never insert fillers under any circumstances.
- That said, do not leave awkward pauses either. Speak with good tempo.

The justification is signal: fillers and overlaps add noise, and a steady tempo keeps the channel readable, the spoken counterpart of the plain prose register in the `prose-style` skill.
