# .github

GitHub's templates and workflows for this repository, added on the user's
instruction. The rules they enforce live in the `issue-tracking` skill (issue
form) and the `rules-version-control` skill, "Pull Requests and Hosting Tools"
(PR form and Actions). Change a template and its skill together.

- `ISSUE_TEMPLATE/` — the issue form; blank issues are off.
- `pull_request_template.md` — the PR body form.
- `workflows/` — checks that refuse what does not follow the templates.
